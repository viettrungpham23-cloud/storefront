# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nhan vien sales va quan tri noi bo cua dai ly VinFast Thu Anh tai Ha Noi. Ho dung ung dung tren dien thoai de tu van nhanh, xem gia va thong so xe, them xe vao gio, lap don cho khach va dong bo ve he thong QLBH.

## Product Purpose

Storefront la app ban hang xe may dien VinFast Thu Anh: hien thi danh muc xe theo nhom doi pin, kem pin, hoc sinh; trinh bay gia ban va thong so ky thuat; ho tro gio hang, thanh toan, quet CCCD va dong bo don sang website quan tri. Thanh cong la sales co the tim dung xe, noi ro gia/loai pin/thong so va chot don khong bi nham du lieu.

## Brand Personality

Ro rang, tin cay, nhanh gon. Giao dien nen tao cam giac nhu mot cong cu ban hang noi bo chuyen nghiep: thuc dung, de quet thong tin, co nhan dien VinFast/Thu Anh nhung khong trang tri qua tay.

## Anti-references

Khong bien app thanh landing page marketing, khong dung hero qua lon, card lap lai thieu thong tin, mau sac loe loet, hieu ung trang tri lam cham thao tac, hoac an thong so quan trong sau nhieu tap.

## Design Principles

1. Gia va loai pin phai hien truoc, khong de sales phai doan.
2. Thong so ky thuat phai du de tu van tai cho, nhung chia nhom de doc nhanh tren man hinh dien thoai.
3. Moi trang thai ban hang nhu dang ban, ngung kinh doanh, het hang phai duoc noi ro ngay tren card va chi tiet.
4. Luong mua hang uu tien thao tac mot tay, nut chinh ro rang, tab/bo loc on dinh.
5. Du lieu hien thi phai khop nguon bang gia va dong bo voi QLBH.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mac dinh theo WCAG AA: tuong phan chu ro tren nen, font khong qua nho trong bang thong so, khong phu thuoc mau sac de hieu trang thai, va ton trong reduced motion. Noi dung tieng Viet phai vua khung tren mobile.
